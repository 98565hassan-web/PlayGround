## PAC-MAN: Memory Maze

A touch-first Pac-Man where the ghosts build a memory of how you move. Every pellet you eat leaves a heat trail in the maze; the ghosts blur that trail into a scent and use it to cut you off on the corridors you favour. Clear a level and they keep a portion of what they learned — each level starts with faster ghosts and a stronger memory of your route.

### Play

- **Swipe** anywhere on the maze, or use the **D-pad** in the bottom corner.
- **Tilt** is optional; on iOS, tapping TILT asks for motion-sensor permission.
- Keyboard: arrows / WASD, `P` or `Esc` to pause, `M` to mute, `T` for tilt.
- Tap **GHOST MEMORY** in the HUD to reveal the route the ghosts have learned.
- Power pellets reverse the hunt: eat ghosts for 200 → 400 → 800 → 1600 points.
- Memory shards appear at 55, 140 and 220 pellets for bonus points.
- 3 lives; an extra life every third level (up to 5).

Everything is a static build — open `index.html` or run `bash start.sh`.
